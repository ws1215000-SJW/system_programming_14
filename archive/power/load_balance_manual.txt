=== POWER LOAD BALANCE MANUAL ===

Equipment: Power Distribution Controller

[ERROR P03]
Power distribution imbalance.

[REQUIRED CONFIGURATION]

supply_generator=40
supply_cooling=60

Total Supply: 100

[RECOVERY]
1. Check the current power distribution.
2. Set generator supply to 40.
3. Set cooling supply to 60.
4. Verify the total supply equals 100.
