=== POWER LOAD BALANCE MANUAL ===

Equipment: Power Distribution Controller

[ERROR P03]
Power distribution imbalance.
Control sees frequency and load balance drop.

[REQUIRED CONFIGURATION]

supply_generator=40
supply_cooling=60

Total Supply: 100

[SPLIT HISTORY]
2023-11-06  generator 75, cooling 25   for PUMP-A (CP-50)
2025-07-02  generator 40, cooling 60   for PUMP-B (CP-70)
2026-03-30  generator 60, cooling 40   entry error, reversed.
            Generator branch went over its limit.
2026-03-30  generator 40, cooling 60   corrected

Commissioning limits (kW)
Bus nominal: 5200. Minimum: 4700 (low supply warning).
Maximum: 5460 (5% above nominal).
GN-2291-K branch, 40%: 1880 to 2184 kW.
PUMP-B branch, 60%: 2820 to 3276 kW.
Check measured branch deliveries after the split has settled.

[FIELD CHECK]
status    in the Power Room: supply power and the kW
          delivered to GN-2291-K and to PUMP-B

[RECOVERY]
1. Check the current power distribution.
2. Set generator supply to 40.
3. Set cooling supply to 60.
   Field: repair power_split <generator> <cooling>
4. Verify the total supply equals 100.
   The branch deliveries need a few seconds to settle.
   The field console waits for them by itself.
