=== BREAKER RESET MANUAL ===

Equipment: BK-7
Location: Power Room

[ERROR P02]
Circuit breaker process failure.
Control sees bus voltage and supply power collapse.

[SAFETY CHECK]
The fault circuit must be isolated
before restarting the breaker.

Required Configuration:
fault_isolated=1

[STEP 1]
Check the power configuration.
Field: cat circuit_status.txt   (which circuit shows fault)
       cat circuit_label.txt    (its circuit ID)

[STEP 2]
Set fault_isolated to 1.
Field: repair fault_isolated <circuit ID> 1
The circuit then shows "isolated". A wrong ID is refused.

[STEP 3]
Check the breaker process state.
Field: ps
  state=STOPPED   suspended, process still exists
  state=DEAD      TERMINATED, process is gone

[STEP 4]
Resume or restart the breaker.
STOPPED: resume breaker
DEAD:    restart breaker
Before step 2 is done both commands are refused.

[STEP 5]
Verify breaker operation.
Supply power needs a few seconds to return. Wait for it.
