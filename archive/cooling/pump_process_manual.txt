=== COOLING PUMP RECOVERY MANUAL ===

Equipment: PUMP-B
Model: CP-70
Location: Cooling Room

[ERROR C02]
Cooling pump process stopped.
Control sees coolant flow at 0 and coolant temperature
climbing.

[STEP 1]
Check the pump process state.
Field: ps
  state=RUNNING   normal
  state=STOPPED   suspended, process still exists
  state=DEAD      TERMINATED, process is gone

[STEP 2]
If the process is STOPPED:
Use "resume pump".

[STEP 3]
If the process is TERMINATED:
Use "restart pump".

[STEP 4]
Check whether the pump is running.
The pid changes after a restart, not after a resume.

[WARNING]
Do not confuse STOPPED with TERMINATED.
The wrong command is refused and the time is lost.

[PAST CASES]
2026-01-14  STOPPED by the watchdog.
            "restart pump" refused. "resume pump" worked.
2026-07-02  TERMINATED after a power dip.
            "resume pump" refused. "restart pump" worked.
