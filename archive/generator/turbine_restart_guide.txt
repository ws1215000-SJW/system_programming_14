=== TURBINE RESTART GUIDE ===

Equipment: Turbine Controller
Location: Generator Room

[STEP 1]
Check the turbine process state.

[STEP 2]
If the process is STOPPED:
Use "resume turbine".

[STEP 3]
If the process is DEAD:
Use "restart turbine".

[STEP 4]
Check the process state again.

[CAUTION]
Use resume for STOPPED and restart for DEAD.
